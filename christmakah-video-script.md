## 1. Final script — clean read-through

> *(Read this straight through to check the rhythm before you build anything.)*

**ACT ONE — the sermon** *(solemn, slow, candlelit)*

December is a month for remembrance.

Hanukkah asks us to remember the peril of our ancestors and the light that got them through it.

Christmas asks us to remember the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the peace he promised the world.

December asks us to be still, and to be grateful.

*(beat)*

**ACT TWO — the turn** *(hard cut, technicolor, fast)*

Just kidding.

December is when a great many religions co-opted some very good pagan traditions, which are:

The Earth turns.
The food will go bad if we don't eat it all now.
Your family demands facetime. Not the app.

So.

**CHRISTMAKAH**
at the Penmars

Sunday, December 13 · 3:00 PM
7505 W Encinas Ln, Phoenix

**THE FOOD**
The digestibles will be of a Jewish nature.

**THE GIFTS**
Aligning with a Secret Santa persuasion — there are rules.

**RULE ONE.** Homemade only. No exceptions. No gift cards. We will know.

**RULE TWO.** You get one randomly selected recipient.

**RULE THREE.** The gift has exactly one job: make that person feel loved, appreciated, and good about themselves.

Acceptable forms include but are not limited to:

— a letter *(if handwritten, make it legible — you know who you are)*
— an AI-generated song about them
— a drawing of them as a superhero
— a flip book
— a papier-mâché of their face

You get the picture.

Sign your work or stay anonymous. Your call.

**THE PRIZE**
A special prize goes to whoever's gift makes their recipient cry.

*(In joy and love, obviously. We will be judging.)*

**YOUR ASSIGNMENT**
Some of you (not the parents) will be asked to bring an appetizer or a dessert. Store-bought is not merely accepted, it is expected.

The kitchen is open, however, should you wish to surprise us.

**THE DRESS CODE**
There will be photographs. Arrive festively attired.

Need an ugly sweater? We keep spares.

**CHRISTMAKAH**
Sunday, December 13 · 3:00 PM

The Earth is turning. The food is going.
Come be together.

RSVP: Your attendance is mandatory. No RSVP required.

## 3. Art direction

Two visual languages, one hard cut between them. This contrast *is* the joke — build it deliberately.

### Act One — "The Sermon" (Scenes 1–4, 0:00–0:14)
- **Background:** near-black midnight blue `#0B1026`, heavy vignette
- **Type:** high-contrast serif (Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond), light weight, wide letter-spacing, centered, small on a big empty field
- **Color:** candle gold `#E8B84B` type on midnight. Nothing else.
- **Motion:** 2.5–4s per line. Slow fade up, slow fade out. Barely-drifting particles or a single flickering flame. Everything *breathes*.
- **Audio:** a single sustained cello note, or solo piano

### Act Two — "The Party" (Scenes 5–18, 0:14–1:10)
- **Background:** cycles hot — cranberry `#C8283C`, pine `#1E6B4F`, menorah blue `#1B4FA0`, cream `#FDF6E3`
- **Type:** chunky geometric sans, heavy weight, big, often set at a slight tilt (−3° to +3°)
- **Accents:** gold foil `#E8B84B`, confetti, six-pointed stars, dreidels, latkes, candy canes, pine sprigs — mixed together with zero reverence
- **Motion:** 1.5–3s per card. Elements pop in with overshoot, slide, or snap. Nothing fades politely.
- **Audio:** klezmer clarinet playing something that is *almost* Jingle Bells

### Rules that apply to both acts
- Safe margins: keep all type at least 120px from every edge (phone UI eats corners)
- Minimum on-screen type size: 48px — this gets watched on a phone, in a group text
- One idea per card. Never two.
- Every card is legible as a still. If someone screenshots scene 8, they have the date and address.

## 5. Copy-paste prompt for Claude Design

Paste everything in the block below into Claude Design. It's written to produce one canvas of 19 vertical artboards you can then refine by hand and export as frames.

```
Build me a 19-artboard vertical storyboard for an animated video invitation
called "Christmakah at the Penmars."

CANVAS
- 19 artboards, each 1080 x 1920 px, laid out left to right in scene order
- Label each artboard "Scene 01" through "Scene 19"
- Keep all text inside a 120px safe margin on every edge
- Minimum text size 48px; hero text 140-220px
- Every artboard must be fully legible as a standalone still image

TONE
Deadpan, warm, tongue-in-cheek family humor. Reverent for four boards, then
a hard comedic turn into loud holiday chaos. The contrast between the two
halves is the entire joke - make it visually violent.

ACT ONE STYLE (Scenes 01-04)
- Background: #0B1026 (near-black midnight blue) with heavy vignette
- Type: high-contrast light-weight serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant
  Garamond), centered, generous letter-spacing, small in a large empty field
- Only accent color: candle gold #E8B84B

ACT TWO STYLE (Scenes 05-19)
- Backgrounds cycle: cranberry #C8283C, pine #1E6B4F, menorah blue #1B4FA0,

- Accent: gold foil #E8B84B, confetti, six-pointed stars, dreidels, latkes,

ARTBOARD CONTENT

  Single small candle flame in the lower third.
Scene 02 - Act One. Text: "Hanukkah asks us to remember the peril our
  ancestors faced - and the light that got them through it."
  Eight faint candle silhouettes along the bottom edge.
Scene 03 - Act One. Text: "Christmas asks us to remember a birth in a stable,

Scene 05 - Act Two. Cranberry. Text: "Just kidding." Enormous, tilted 3
```

### Then, to animate

Once the boards look right, hand Claude Design the motion pass:

```
Animate this storyboard as a ~70 second vertical video.

Scenes 01-04: slow and reverent. 3-4 seconds each. Type fades up over 1.2s
and fades out. Candle flames flicker gently. End Scene 04 with a full 1.5
seconds of empty frame before the cut - do not shorten this pause, the
comedy depends on it.

Scene 05: HARD CUT, no transition. Text slams in with overshoot, confetti
bursts outward.

Scenes 06-18: fast and punchy, 3-5 seconds each. Elements pop in with
overshoot or snap into place. Never fade politely. In Scene 07 the three
lines land one at a time, ~1.2s apart. In Scene 11, "We will know." appears
a full beat after the rest of the rule. In Scene 14, items land rapidly at
about 0.9s each.

Scene 08 and Scene 19: hold 5-8 seconds. These are the cards people will
screenshot for the date and address.

Scene 19: everything settles, confetti drifts down and stops, candle keeps
flickering through the hold.

Audio: solo cello or piano under Act One; a klezmer clarinet playing
something that is almost but not quite Jingle Bells under Act Two. Record
scratch on the cut into Scene 05.
```
